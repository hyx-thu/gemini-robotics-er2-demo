Quick Start & Demo Guide
⚡ How It Works (Human-in-the-Loop Simulation)
  1. Select Embodiment & Model: Choose Spot at the top center, and select gemini-robotics-er-2 or gemini-3.8-live below the video feed.
  2. Connect & Speak: Click the Power button to start the session and webcam, then enable the Microphone to give voice commands (or type in the chat panel).
  3. Manually Simulate Robot Actions: When the model issues tool commands such as detect, pick, or place (shown on the video overlay & Plan HUD), please manually act as the robot arm by picking up or moving the physical object in front of the camera as instructed. The model continuously observes the live video feed to visually verify that the action succeeded before advancing to the next step in its plan!
